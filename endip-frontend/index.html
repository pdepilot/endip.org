Mission

A centre for enterprise and human capacity

Our focus is on capacity building for youths, women and men through leadership training, vocational skills programmes, micro-enterprise support and entrepreneurship education.

By offering support to vulnerable individuals in Nigeria, ENDIP works so that skills meet opportunity — in workplaces, schools, communities and enterprises. The 2030 goal is to build 10,000 sustainable enterprises and contribute to 30,000 jobs through capacity development, micro-credit and business incubation.
