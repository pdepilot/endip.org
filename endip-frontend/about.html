Who we are

Entrepreneurial Development Initiative

People with skills build the world. ENDIP exists to turn that conviction into internships, enterprises, training and inclusive opportunity in Nigeria.

Mission

Our focus is on capacity building for youths, women, and men through leadership training, vocational skills programmes, micro-enterprise support, and entrepreneurship education. By offering support to vulnerable individuals in Nigeria, ENDIP works to harness the creative and business potential of young people for national prosperity.

Vision

A Nigeria where skilled people, sustainable enterprises and inclusive opportunity drive prosperity.

2030 goal

Building 10,000 sustainable enterprises to create 30,000 jobs in Nigeria by 2030 through capacity development, micro-credit, and business incubation.

How ENDIP works

  1. Publish programmes and opportunities with clear eligibility.
  2. Select and support participants through structured training or placement.
  3. Work with hosts, schools and enterprises where partnerships exist.
  4. Record learning, attendance and completion — then keep alumni and businesses in a trusted network.

Areas of intervention

  • Business support
  • Education and human resource development
  • Economic and local development
  • Social development
  • Vocational training
